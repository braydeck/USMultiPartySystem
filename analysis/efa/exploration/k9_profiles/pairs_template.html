The two closest pairs in the nine-party typology, side by side: Civic Union against Conservative, and Labor against Liberal. Figures are survey-weighted shares of each party's members (45,214 respondents, branch bd-nine-parties). "All" is the national figure. House seats are the default view (873 seats).

Name options for the Liberal party

The party is progressive on taxes, climate, abortion and guns, at or above the national average on policing and border patrols, the most trusting of elections of any party, and interventionist abroad (75% back arming Ukraine). It is the oldest party, median age 62. "Liberal" in US usage reads as simply left of center, which covers the first half of that profile and misses the second.

Codes are checked against the existing ones (CON, LBR, STY, NAT, LIB, POP, CUP, DSA, PRG). Renaming touches party labels across the app and the data payloads, so it is best settled before the party copy goes in.
