# CES 2024 team modules: items for profiling clusters on priorities and intensity

Scanned 2026-10-27. Sources were the codebook (`CCES24_XXX_CODEBOOK.txt`) and the pre- and post-election questionnaires (`CCES24_XXX.docx`, `CCES24_XXX_post.docx`) for each module. No data files were opened. Local copies of the documents are in the session scratchpad (`ces_modules/`).

**Release status: released.** All 46 team modules were published to the Harvard Dataverse CCES collection on 2026-10-05, titled "CES 2024, Team Module of …". Each entry holds an `_OUTPUT.tab` data file, a text codebook, and pre and post questionnaires. YLS is the exception: it has a codebook only and no questionnaire. Every codebook header gives `Interviews: 1000`. Items marked "post" were asked only of respondents who took the post-election wave.

**Variable-prefix traps for the merge.**
- IOW variables are named `Q1`–`Q55`.
- MSL (Missouri–St. Louis) variables use the `UMS` prefix, and so do UMS (Mississippi) variables. For example, `UMS301` means different items in the two modules.
- UNC (Charlotte) variables use the `NCC` prefix.
- UTB variables use the `UTA` prefix, and so do UTA variables.
- Keep the module code (`ccesmodule`) alongside every variable name.

**Common Content already has some foreign-policy items.** `CC24_308a_1`–`_8` cover Ukraine and `CC24_308b_*` cover Israel/Gaza, as select-all lists of US responses. `CC24_420_*` covers uses of US troops.

Categories: **P** = issue importance, priority, trade-off or conjoint. **F** = foreign policy or internationalism. **T** = institutional trust or election confidence. **A** = authoritarianism, populism, anti-elite attitudes or egalitarianism.

## Checked and not relevant

- **BFL** (Buffalo): honor, apocalypticism, and a yes/no issue grid `BFL401`–`412` that duplicates the Common Content format.
- **CHI** (Chicago): executive power, rule of law, Supreme Court decisions.
- **DAR** (Dartmouth): Medicare and Social Security knowledge experiments, lottery.

- **OXF** (Oxford): personal economics and income. `OXF355` (support for redistribution) is a policy preference, not an attitude battery.
- **UTB** (UT Austin, `UTA*` vars): primary participation, health-care and immigration framing experiments.
- **YAL** (Yale, YAL): media, capital-gains tax, housing, politician ratings. `YAL433` ranks priorities within immigration only.
