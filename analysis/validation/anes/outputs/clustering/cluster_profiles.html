ANES 2024 clusters, two ways

Matched clusters run the production CES chain on the 18 items ANES shares with the CES typology (n = 3,796). Independent clusters use ANES's own 10 factors from its 96-item screened pool (n = 2,655, respondents complete on all 96). Both use the same DPGMM, capped at 9 clusters; respondents in components holding under 1% of the weight are left unassigned (0 matched, 42 independent). Rows are ordered from most Democratic to most Republican party ID.
