ANES 2024 clusters, two ways

Matched clusters run the production CES chain on the 18 items ANES shares with the CES typology (n = 3,796). Independent clusters use ANES's own ten factors on all 99 screened items, each respondent scored on the items they answered (n = 4,673, respondents who answered at least half of each factor's items), with the cluster count chosen on seed and resample stability (4 clusters). Matched clusters use the DPGMM capped at 9; respondents in components holding under 1% of the weight are left unassigned (0 matched, 0 independent). Rows are ordered from most Democratic to most Republican party ID.
